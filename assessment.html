What useful evidence looks like

Four evidence areas

These areas help you select evidence for feedback. They are not a marking rubric and do not create a formal task.

Keep these boundaries clear

  • Folio text, imported images, backups and exports remain preparation until submitted through a teacher-approved route.
  • Knowledge checks and activities provide practice and feedback. They are not automatically copied into the folio or converted into a formal mark.
  • A screenshot, filename or website link does not prove teacher access, submission or teacher-observed performance.
  • Do not include faces, names, email addresses, account menus, comments or private sharing details in public-facing evidence.
