## Preservation and placement contract

- Source images in `assets/source/` are unchanged copies of the authorised Drive bytes. Do not crop, annotate over or replace them.
- Use `object-fit: contain` for source artefacts, diagrams and any visual whose whole shape teaches.
- Every placement must render its nearby caption, **Notice** prompt and keyboard-operable **Open larger** control.
- Full-resolution `Open larger` uses the unchanged path recorded below.
- One visual may be reused by its matching module presentation and still counts once. Any other reuse needs a separate recorded teaching purpose.
- Generated photos are project-created gap assets, not student work or documentary evidence.
- The five generated photos used the built-in image tool. No CLI/API fallback or external reference image was used.
