## Confirmed project contract from Drive

Students develop an original fast-food business and a connected digital brand. The source pack supports this production sequence:

1. define the business name, slogan, products, brand colours and target audience;
2. apply graphic-design vocabulary: typography, colour palette, visual hierarchy and mood boards;
3. create an original logo in Canva;
4. organise product/menu information and study the supplied WWHS canteen menu as an information-design example, not as current price authority;
5. create promotional posters and contextual mock-ups;
6. apply the visual identity to a staff uniform;
7. create animated digital advertising using Canva's Match and Move workflow and export MP4 evidence;
8. investigate marketing, target audiences, greenwashing, business systems and ethical responsibility through the supplied simulation scaffold;
9. build a Google Sites portfolio with Home, Products and Contact Us pages, displaying the brand's graphics and videos;
10. submit the website link and evaluate the work and learning experience.

The Drive form calls this a **Multimedia & Marketing** unit and refers to work completed "this term". A one-term sequence is a strong inference, not a confirmed timetable.

| 4. Menu information design | Grouping and information architecture; Readability and scanning; Honest product information | Structured menu draft |

| 6. Brand applications | Consistent identity; Designing a staff uniform; Checking suitability and accessibility | Uniform concept and annotation |

| 9. Web portfolio | Site structure and navigation; Preparing and embedding media; Publishing, access and submission | Three-page Google Sites portfolio and link |
| 10. Present, evaluate, improve | Success criteria and feedback; Explaining design decisions; Evaluation and next iteration | Presentation, evaluation and backup/export |
