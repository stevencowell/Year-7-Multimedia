## Scope and authority

This handoff backward-maps the current local Year 7 Technology — Multimedia: Fast Food Futures candidate. It inspected the actual ten modules, 30 named theory sections, 90 formative knowledge checks, 30 section capstones, 19 Applied Learning Activities, 35 teaching visuals, six Drive videos, eight folio stages and the formative assessment-status handoff.

The program content uses the **Technology 7–8 Syllabus (2023)** for 2026 delivery and the **Digital and communication technologies** focus area. It is school-developed and NESA-aligned; it does not claim NESA approval or endorsement.

The final evidenced outcome subset is:

- `TE4-SDP-01` — explains relationships between sustainability, design and production
- `TE4-PDP-01` — describes the practices and processes of designers and producers
- `TE4-PPM-01` — applies processes in the planning, management and production of projects
- `TE4-DES-01` — communicates and evaluates design ideas and solutions
- `TE4-SAF-01` — selects and safely uses tools, materials, technologies and processes
- `TE4-DIG-01` — demonstrates technological literacy to safely interact in digital environments
- `TE4-DIG-02` — uses data and digital systems to code, design and produce projects

`TE4-MSC-01` was removed from the Stage 01 provisional set because the integrated unit does not contain a distinct, source-supported materials, systems and components learning sequence strong enough to warrant selecting the outcome.
